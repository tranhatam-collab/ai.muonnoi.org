# AI Muôn Nơi Autonomous Operating Kit

## Purpose and current boundary

This is the executable commercial and editorial kit for the AI Muôn Nơi master direction: AI assistance, a reviewed Publishing Studio, and approved workflows. It is not evidence of a production release, a paid order, a customer account, or permission to publish automatically.

`app.muonnoi.org` remains the authority for Social Core. `ai.muonnoi.org` owns drafts, assistance, verification, approval records, and the Studio workflow. RevenueOps is an internal, zero-budget module until payment, consent, security and reconciliation gates are evidenced.

## Commercial offer sequence

The draft catalog in `data/offer-catalog.json` is intentionally limited to four offers. The first sale is a 14-day Studio Pilot because it proves value without promising autonomous public posting or relying on a social graph migration.

1. Convert useful public or partner content into a qualified Pilot request.
2. Create a scoped proposal with the buyer's approval, deliverables, price and due date.
3. Create a payment intent only after the payment gate is released.
4. Count revenue only after a successful verified payment event and reconciliation record.
5. Deliver reviewed assets, collect outcome feedback, then offer the appropriate recurring plan.

The planning scenario is five Pilot orders (7,500,000 VND), three Creator subscriptions and two Team subscriptions (15,950,000 VND monthly recurring), plus two B2B workflow set-ups (19,800,000 VND). This is a target scenario, never an actuals field.

## Ten operating roles

| Role | Daily output | Non-negotiable control |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinator | prioritized workboard and gate state | preserves source and lane identity |
| Research | cited buyer and topic insight | no invented facts |
| Content strategy | brief, audience, objective, CTA | matches offer and consent |
| Script writer | video, carousel, article and landing drafts | drafts are versioned |
| Verifier | source/claim review and uncertainty label | no AI-created verified claim |
| Brand editor | Vietnamese voice and readability review | no deceptive promise |
| Studio runner | drafts, job history and approval state | no external publication |
| Distribution planner | channel-specific package and UTM plan | account ownership required |
| RevenueOps | lead, proposal, invoice and cash ledger | paid is reconciled, not predicted |
| Reliability operator | quota, incidents, backup and rollback receipt | stop on missing critical gate |

## Operating cadence

| Cadence | Output |
|---|---|
| Daily | source/live gate check, content drafts, task completion, brief operating receipt |
| Weekly | seven-day queue, buyer funnel, verified cash/cost reconciliation and priority reset |
| Monthly | retention, margin, reliability, security and roadmap decision |
| Annual | strategy, resilience, access, retention, legal and capacity review |

## Stop conditions

The automation must stop the affected external action, record the reason, and continue safe internal work when any of these are true:

- no configured account owner, destination or approval policy exists;
- payment callback, reconciliation or email control plane is unverified;
- consent, rights, claims or source evidence is missing;
- a budget, quota or provider error occurs;
- the source/deployment identity is ambiguous or another active lane would be overwritten;
- a rollback, backup or incident response gate is required but lacks evidence.
